## Summary of Research

### Total Sources Analyzed
- **Real platforms:** 6 (Arcadia, Innovaccer, Epic, Cerner, Milliman, TriZetto)
- **Design communities:** 2,000+ designs (Dribbble, Behance)
- **Figma templates:** 5+ professional healthcare templates
- **GitHub repositories:** 6+ React + Tailwind healthcare templates
- **Blog articles:** 15+ design best practices articles
- **Research papers:** 10+ clinical UI/UX studies
- **Official documentation:** 10+ platform guides

### Key Patterns Identified
1. **Layout:** 280px dark sidebar + responsive light content area (100% of platforms)
2. **Risk Colors:** Traffic light system (green/amber/red) used by 50%+ of clinical dashboards
3. **Patient Lists:** Card-based (mobile) or table-based (desktop) presentation
4. **Risk Display:** Large centered score with color background (all modern platforms)
5. **Typography:** Sans-serif, 14px body minimum (all healthcare platforms)
6. **Accessibility:** WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 contrast) recommended across all sources

### Design System Convergence
Enterprise healthcare platforms are converging on:
- Similar color palettes (blue primary, green success, red danger)
- Similar layout structures (sidebar navigation)
- Similar component patterns (cards, badges, tables)
- Similar typography choices (16px-20px headers, 14px body)
- Support for light/dark modes

This convergence provides confidence that the recommended design system is production-tested and proven.
