KEY METRICS TRACKED
==============================================================================

Executive Level (Revenue/Performance):
  - Total RAF Points (sum of all member RAFs)
  - Average Member RAF (plan/provider/region benchmarking)
  - Estimated Annual Revenue (RAF × $10,400/0.1)
  - Revenue YoY Trend (forecasting)
  - Actuarial variance (projection vs actual)

Operational Level (Quality/Productivity):
  - Gap Closure Rate (% identified gaps closed)
  - Days to Gap Closure (median, 90th percentile)
  - Coder Productivity (members reviewed/day, HCCs per review)
  - Data Quality Score (composite: completeness, accuracy)
  - Claim Acceptance Rate (rejected claims, reasons)
  - Provider Engagement (closing gaps / assigned gaps)

Actuarial Level (Financial Impact):
  - Coding Intensity Ratio (actual RAF / expected RAF)
  - CMS Sweep Reconciliation (adjustment amounts)
  - Bid Forecast Accuracy (projected vs actual RAF)
  - STARS Impact (estimated bonus $)
  - MLR (medical loss ratio) impact
