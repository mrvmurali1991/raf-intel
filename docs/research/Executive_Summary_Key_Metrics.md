## COMPLIANCE ROADMAP (CONDENSED)

### Must-Have Before Launch
- [ ] **Business Associate Agreement (BAA)** - Legal requirement; mandatory before handling PHI
- [ ] **HIPAA Compliance Program** - Policies, training, risk assessment
- [ ] **Data Security Infrastructure** - Encryption, access controls, audit logging

### Critical Path to Series A
- [ ] **SOC 2 Type II** (12 months)
  - Cost: $20K-$60K
  - Impact: Enterprise credibility
  - Timeline: Month 2-12

- [ ] **HIPAA Risk Assessment** (Annual)
  - Cost: $5K-$15K
  - Impact: Demonstrates due diligence
  - Timeline: Month 0-6

### Competitive Advantage (Year 2)
- [ ] **HITRUST Certification** (18 months)
  - Cost: $60K-$150K
  - Impact: Premium pricing (10-15% higher)
  - Timeline: Month 12-18

## COMPETITIVE POSITIONING

### Avoid These Mistakes
- Don't position as "better Apixio" (you can't match their AI/funding)
- Don't target enterprise only (focus: mid-market)
- Don't ignore partnerships (direct sales CAC too high)
- Don't launch without HIPAA/BAA foundation (deal-breaker for every customer)
- Don't ignore first 90-day CS (80% of retention determined here)

### Your Competitive Advantages
1. **Speed:** 30-45 day implementation vs. 90+ days competitors
2. **Price:** Transparent, lower PMPM ($1.00-$1.50) vs. black-box custom pricing
3. **Simplicity:** Built for mid-market complexity, not enterprise bloat
4. **Modern tech:** Cloud-native, API-first vs. legacy architectures
5. **Channel access:** Partner-sourced customers = lower CAC

## DECISION FRAMEWORK: BUILD OR PARTNER?

### If You're Building...
**Minimum viable checklist:**
1. Can you raise $200K-$500K seed? (Legal, compliance, engineering 6-12 months)
2. Do you have health IT domain expertise? (Risk adjustment knowledge = critical)
3. Are you willing to focus: mid-market ONLY, no enterprise initially?
4. Can you accept 12-18 month path to Series A? (Slow sales cycle)
5. Do you have compliance tolerance? (HIPAA/BAA/SOC 2 = non-negotiable)

### If You're Considering Partnership...
**Partner-first approach checklist:**
1. Approach established consulting firms (Guidehouse, Deloitte, 3Gen)
2. Offer: white-label platform + their consulting services
3. Benefit: faster customer acquisition (consulting firm referrals)
4. Trade-off: lower per-unit margin (25-35% revenue share)
5. Timeline: 6-12 months to first customers through partner channel

## CONCLUSION

**The risk adjustment market is a compelling opportunity for a focused, well-executed startup:**

**Why it's attractive:**
- Regulatory tailwind ($40B overpayment exposure)
- Clear ROI (customer quantifiable value = $141-$282/member/year)
- Incumbent vendors not optimized for mid-market
- Fast implementation (30-90 days enables quick sales cycles)
- Expansion opportunity (audit, quality, member management)

**Why it's hard:**
- Long sales cycles (6-12 months for health plans)
- Complex compliance (HIPAA/BAA/SOC 2 mandatory)
- Entrenched competitors (Apixio, Reveleer, Cotiviti)
- Integration challenges (EHR connectivity is hard)
- Small early customer base (first 10 customers are foundational)

**Success factors:**
1. **Focus ruthlessly** on mid-market (100K-500K member plans), not enterprise
2. **Speed is competitive advantage** - 30-45 day implementations vs. 90+ days
3. **Build partnerships early** - Lower CAC, faster customer acquisition
4. **Invest in customer success** - 80% of retention is first 90 days
5. **Compliance foundation first** - HIPAA/BAA before launch, SOC 2 by Series A, HITRUST for premium positioning

**3-Year target:**
- 80-120 customers
- $3M-$5M ARR
- 78-80% gross margins
- Clear acquisition target or public trajectory

**Estimated capital required:** $2M-$7M to reach $4M+ ARR (Series A + growth)
**Timeline to profitability:** 24-30 months from launch
