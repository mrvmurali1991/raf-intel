| UnitedHealth | Pending | Ongoing | DOJ criminal/civil investigation |

### Documentation Standard (MEAT Framework)
- **M**onitoring: Active ongoing monitoring of condition
- **E**valuation: Clinical evaluation and assessment
- **A**ssessment: Documented clinical assessment present
- **T**reatment: Treatment plan linked to disease

**Compliance Rate:** Historical audits invalidate 20-40% of unsupported codes

### CMS-HCC Model Transition
- 2024: 67% current + 33% V28
- 2025: 33% current + 67% V28
- 2026: 100% V28 (current status)

### Court Ruling: Humana v. Becerra
- **Date:** September 25, 2025
- **Decision:** Vacated CMS-1733-F on APA procedural grounds
- **Status:** CMS appealed November 21, 2025
- **Impact:** Extrapolation methodology in legal limbo pending appeal
