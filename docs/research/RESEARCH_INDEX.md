## Key Findings Summary

### Primary Data Sources Beyond Billing Codes (Ranked by Impact)

**Tier 1: Highest Impact (30-40% of missed HCCs)**
1. Laboratory Values (eGFR, HbA1c, BNP) - Objective, verifiable
2. Medication Reconciliation - Medications reveal diagnoses
3. BMI/Vital Signs - Supports condition specificity

**Tier 2: Strong Support (15-20% of missed HCCs)**
4. Problem Lists (when current and clean) - Foundation for MEAT
5. Specialist Referral Data - Uncovers complex conditions
6. Visit Patterns/Frequency - Indicates disease management

**Tier 3: Contextual Value (5-10% of missed HCCs)**
7. SDOH Data - Future regulatory importance
8. Immunization Records - Quality alignment
9. Historical Trends - Condition recapture
10. Member Self-Report - HRA data validation

---

### Critical Regulatory Changes (2025-2026)

**RAPS Transition Completed:**
- 100% migration to EDPS (Encounter Data Processing System)
- Stricter validation rules (7 layers vs. RAPS 2 layers)
- Average 11.9% revenue impact if not prepared

**CMS V28 Implementation:**
- 100% of 2025+ DOS use V28 model
- Requires much greater specificity in documentation
- 115 HCCs (vs. 86 previously); 29 new categories

**RADV Audit Expansion:**
- 550+ plans audited annually (vs. 60 previously)
- 200 records per contract (vs. 35 previously)
- Bidirectional validation (add AND remove unsupported)
- Aetna/CVS $117.7M settlement (March 2026) signals active enforcement
