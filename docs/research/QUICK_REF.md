## CRITICAL DEFINITIONS

**MEAT Criteria:** Monitor, Evaluate, Assess/Address, Treat (required proof for all HCC diagnoses)

**HCC:** Hierarchical Condition Category recognized by CMS for risk adjustment payment

**RAF:** Risk Adjustment Factor multiplier on base capitation (each HCC worth $141-$282+ PMPY)

**Concurrent:** Review during/immediately after encounter, before claims submission

**Prospective:** Identify suspected conditions before encounter, real-time point-of-care coding

## QUICK SELECTION GUIDE

**Modern EHR + Provider buy-in?** → **PROSPECTIVE** (highest ROI, lowest risk, fastest cash)

**Large complex organization?** → **HYBRID** (balanced flexibility, 70% prospective + 30% retrospective)

**Legacy systems/tight budget?** → **RETROSPECTIVE** (proven ROI, but slow cash flow and higher audit risk)

**Quality metrics priority?** → **CONCURRENT** (80-90% accuracy, real-time feedback)

## TOP RED FLAGS FOR RADV AUDITORS

1. Back-dated addendums (>30 days post-visit)
2. Bulk amendments clustered before payment deadlines
3. Heavy provider queries (suggests weak documentation)
4. Diagnoses without documented treatment/medication
5. Sudden spikes in HCC coding patterns

**Best Defense:** Prospective documentation at point-of-care (strong audit trail)
