### CMS-HCC Model V28 Analysis

1. **V28 Model Overview & Changes**
   - 115 HCC categories (up from 86)
   - 7,770 valid diagnostic codes (down from 9,797 → 2,000+ codes removed)
   - Fully operative: January 1, 2026
   - Projected risk score reduction: 3.12%
   - Financial impact: ~$1.2M per 10,000-patient plan
   - Sources:
     - [CMS-HCC Model V28: Full List of Chronic Conditions](https://www.raapidinc.com/blogs/cms-hcc-model-v28/)
     - [Trends, Patterns, and Key Comparisons Related to CMS-HCC V24 vs V28](https://oig.hhs.gov/reports/work-plan/browse-work-plan-projects/trends-patterns-and-key-comparisons-related-to-cms-hcc-risk-adjustment-2020-model-v24-and-2024-model-v28/)

2. **Transition Timeline**
   - PY 2024: 33% V28 / 67% V24
   - PY 2025: 67% V28 / 33% V24
   - PY 2026+: 100% V28
   - Source: [Navigating the Transition to V28](https://www.navina.ai/articles/navigating-the-transition-to-v28-impact-in-2025)

3. **Detailed Changes**
   - CHF now split into 5 severity categories (vs. 1 in V24)
   - Diabetes values decreased
   - More specificity required for defensibility
   - Source: [From HCC V24 to V28: Key Changes](https://bluebrix.health/blogs/from-hcc-v24-to-v28-what-billing-team-needs-to-know-right-now)

## Credibility Notes

### High-Credibility Sources in This Research
- **KLAS Research**: Healthcare industry standard for vendor satisfaction evaluation
- **CMS/OIG**: Government sources on audit findings, error rates, policy
- **Press releases**: Direct company announcements (funding, product launches)
- **Venture capital firms**: Bessemer, Menlo, ALIVE (healthcare VC expertise)
- **Industry analysts**: HITINSIGHT, Medical Economics (healthcare media)

### Moderate-Credibility Sources
- Vendor marketing materials (verify with independent sources)
- Third-party review sites (may have bias)
- Market research reports (often proprietary, varying methodologies)

### Cross-Verification Recommended
- Funding amounts: Cross-check press releases with VC announcements
- Customer counts: Compare vendor claims with KLAS/analyst reports
- Accuracy claims: Look for independent validation, not vendor claims alone
- Market size: Multiple sources provide different projections; triangulate
