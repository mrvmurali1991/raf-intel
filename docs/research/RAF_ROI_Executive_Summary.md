## RADV AUDIT RISK & RECOUPMENT ANALYSIS

### Audit Exposure by Approach

**Retrospective-Only Risk Profile:**
```
Audit selection probability: 25-30% annually
Recoupment rate if audited: 10-15% of submitted diagnoses
Average overpayment per audit: $500K-$2.5M
Multi-year audit exposure (3 years): $5M-$12.5M potential

Risk Factors:
• Back-dated addendums attract scrutiny
• Query-driven documentation less defensible
• Timing patterns (amendment spikes) flagged
• Documentation timing weakness

Mitigation:
• Conservative interpretation of queries
• Limit amendment frequency
• Strong MEAT criteria validation
• RADV-readiness audits quarterly
```

**Prospective-Only Risk Profile:**
```
Audit selection probability: 15-20% annually
Recoupment rate if audited: 3-5% of submitted diagnoses
Average overpayment per audit: $75K-$250K
Multi-year audit exposure (3 years): $1M-$3.75M potential

Risk Factors:
• Time-of-care documentation defensible
• Real-time capture reduces amendment patterns
• Provider engagement demonstrates intentionality
• Alert-driven validation creates audit trail

Mitigation:
• Emphasize point-of-care timing
• Maintain audit trail of all alerts/validations
• Provider training documentation
• Transparent workflows for auditors
```

**Hybrid Risk Profile:**
```
Audit selection probability: 20-25% annually
Recoupment rate if audited: 5-8% of submitted diagnoses
Average overpayment per audit: $250K-$1M
Multi-year audit exposure (3 years): $2.5M-$7.5M potential

Risk Factors:
• Blended documentation timing
• Prospective portion defensible
• Retrospective portion higher risk
• Requires clear separation of methodologies

Mitigation:
• Clear prospective/retrospective separation
• Conservative retrospective scope
• Strong overall compliance governance
• Transparent reporting to auditors
```

## RECOMMENDATION FRAMEWORK

### Choose RETROSPECTIVE-ONLY if:
- Budget is extremely constrained
- EHR integration is impossible or prohibitively expensive
- Provider adoption of prospective alerts is low
- Legacy systems will persist for 3+ years
- Acceptable RADV audit risk tolerance
- **Financial fit: Lowest upfront, highest long-term risk**

### Choose PROSPECTIVE-ONLY if:
- Modern EHR with API capabilities exists
- Provider engagement can be secured
- Priority is faster cash flow and working capital
- RADV audit risk reduction critical
- 5-year horizon for planning
- **Financial fit: Highest ROI, lowest risk, fastest payback**

### Choose HYBRID if:
- Need balanced approach with flexibility
- Retrospective team has expertise to preserve
- Want faster cash flow than retrospective alone
- Lower risk tolerance (want fallback options)
- Competitive market (multiple levers needed)
- **Financial fit: Moderate ROI, balanced risk/reward**
