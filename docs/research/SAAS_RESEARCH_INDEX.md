**Analyst Sources:**
- Arcadia, Wolters Kluwer, Guidehouse on risk adjustment market

### Research Gaps & Limitations

1. **Specific competitor pricing:** Most vendors use confidential custom pricing
2. **Exact market size:** Healthcare TAM estimates vary by source; ranges presented are conservative
3. **Regional variations:** Pricing and sales cycles vary by geography (Northeast, Southwest, etc.)
4. **Medicaid complexity:** Research focused on Medicare Advantage; Medicaid has different dynamics
5. **Post-Series A data:** Limited published information on post-Series A burn rates and scaling costs
