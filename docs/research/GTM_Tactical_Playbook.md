```

accelerate HCC coding and close documentation gaps.

Would be worth a 15-minute call to explore if it's relevant?

[Your name]
```

**Expected response rate:** 5-10% acceptance rate

## SECTION 3: DISCOVERY CALL FRAMEWORK (30 MINUTES)

### Pre-Call Preparation (5 minutes)

**Research the prospect:**
- [ ] Review their health plan website (member demographics, service area)
- [ ] Check LinkedIn for VP RA background (prior companies, affiliations)
- [ ] Search for press releases (new products, expansion, funding)
- [ ] Note specific talking points (e.g., "saw you're expanding in Texas")

**Prepare your intro:**
- [ ] 30-second pitch (problem + solution + unique advantage)
- [ ] 2-3 specific questions about their current state
- [ ] ROI calculator (pre-filled with their estimated numbers)

---

### Discovery Call Agenda (30 minutes)

#### MINUTES 0-2: Rapport & Context Setting

**Your opening:**
```
"Thanks for taking 30 minutes. Quick context: we help health plans 
like [Plan Name] close documentation gaps and improve HCC capture 
through software that integrates with your current workflows.

Before I pitch, I'd like to understand your current approach to 
risk adjustment—specifically where you see the biggest gaps.

Sound good?"
```

**Key: Position as learner, not seller**

---

#### MINUTES 2-10: Situation Assessment

**Ask these questions in order:**

1. **"Walk me through your current HCC workflow"**
   - How they identify missed diagnoses
   - Manual vs. automated processes
   - Staffing (coders, QA, management)
   - Pain points (time, accuracy, coverage)

2. **"What's your biggest challenge right now?"**
   - Documentation gaps?
   - Coding accuracy?
   - Staffing/productivity?
   - Provider resistance?

3. **"How do you measure success in HCC?"**
   - RAF scores?
   - Coding accuracy rates?
   - ROI calculations?
   - Compliance metrics (RADV readiness)?

4. **"What have you tried before?"** (if anything)
   - Manual processes?
   - Previous software (why abandoned)?
   - Consulting engagements?
   - Results?

**Listen more than talk (70/30 rule)**

---

#### MINUTES 10-18: Present Solution

**Start with their problem, NOT your product:**

```
"Based on what you described, it sounds like [specific problem] 
is your biggest lever for [specific outcome].

That's actually where we see the most impact. Here's how we typically 
help plans like yours:

1. Identify missed diagnoses (AI + coding expertise)
2. Flag documentation gaps to providers
3. Track coding accuracy and trends
4. Generate reports for management/compliance

For a plan your size [X members], we typically see:
- 2-3 additional codes per patient identified
- 0.5-2% RAF score improvement
- Documentation accuracy improving to 95%+
- 30-45 day implementation

The financial impact: $[X]—$[Y] per year based on your member count.

How does that compare to where you want to be?"
```

**Key points:**
- Lead with their problem
- Quantify the outcome (not features)
- Reference their specific situation
- Ask for reaction ("Does that resonate?")

---

#### MINUTES 18-25: Probe Deeper (Gating Questions)

**Determine if they're ready to move forward:**

1. **Budget:** "Do you have budget allocated for risk adjustment improvements this year?"
   - If no: "No problem—let's keep in touch for next budget cycle"
   - If yes: "Ballpark—are we talking $10K, $50K, $100K+?"

2. **Timeline:** "If this was a fit, what would your implementation look like timeline-wise?"
   - If "not until Q3 2026": Not an immediate prospect
   - If "ASAP": Hot lead, move to pilot

3. **Decision-making:** "Who else would need to sign off on this—CFO, CMO, Chief Compliance?"
   - Map the DMU (Decision Making Unit)
   - Plan next steps with other stakeholders

4. **Precedent:** "Have you implemented new technology before? How did that go?"
   - Understand their risk tolerance
   - Gauge implementation readiness

---

#### MINUTES 25-30: Advance the Sale

**Close with next step (NO ambiguity):**

**If hot lead:**
```
"Based on our conversation, this seems like a fit. Here's what I'd suggest:

1. I'll prepare a 14-day pilot proposal using your specific data
2. You loop in [CMO/CFO] to discuss budget and timeline
3. We schedule a follow-up in one week with your full team

Does that work? Let me send over a quick survey so I can tailor the pilot 
to your specific situation."
```

**If warm lead:**
```
"This is definitely worth exploring further. Here's what I'd suggest:

1. Let me send you our benchmark report for health plans your size
2. You review it and see if there's alignment with your priorities
3. We reconnect in 3-4 weeks to discuss next steps

Fair?"
```

**If cold lead:**
```
"Thanks for your time. Sounds like timing isn't quite right. Let's stay 
in touch—I'll send over relevant content as CMS-HCC updates come out.

When would be a better time to reconnect?"
```

## SECTION 5: CUSTOMER OBJECTION HANDLING

### Top 5 Objections & Responses

---

#### OBJECTION 1: "We're already using [competitor]"

**Your response:**

"That's great—many of the plans we work with had prior solutions. 
Most find that [competitor product] is strong at X, but leaves gaps in Y.

Quick question: Are you getting the ROI you expected? Specifically, 
how much did your RAF improve in the last 6-12 months?"

**[Listen for their response]**

"That's helpful to know. What we see from other plans moving from 
[competitor] to us is usually:

1. Faster implementation (we do 30-45 days, they took 90+)
2. Better diagnosis capture (we average 2-3 more codes per patient)
3. Lower cost (transparent PMPM vs. their markup model)

Rather than asking you to switch now, would it be worth a 14-day pilot 
to see if there's a meaningful difference? No cost, no risk."

**Key point:** Don't trash-talk competitors. Lead with their gaps, your advantages, proof via pilot.

---

#### OBJECTION 2: "Your pricing seems high"

**Your response:**

"I understand—let's talk specifics. What are you currently spending 
on HCC/risk adjustment annually? That includes:
- Software licenses
- Consulting/implementation
- Staff time (FTE costs)
- Audit/compliance"

**[Get their number]**

"Got it, so $X annually. Here's what we typically see:

If your plan has 250K members:
- Each 1% RAF improvement = $250K-$500K value
- Our fee: $450K/year at $1.50 PMPM
- Net value in Year 1: $100K-$250K positive

But more importantly, you don't pay us if we don't deliver. 
Want to do a 30-day implementation with a specific RAF target? 
If we hit it, we negotiate the long-term contract. 
If we don't, no commitment."

**Key point:** Reframe as investment, not cost. Tie to specific value.

---

#### OBJECTION 3: "We don't have budget right now"

**Your response:**

"Totally understand—enterprise budgets are tight. Two questions:

1. When does your next budget cycle open? 
   - Q2? Q3? We should mark time to reconnect

2. If you *did* have budget, would this be a priority?
   - If yes: Let's stay in touch; I'll send relevant content (V28 updates, benchmarks)
   - If no: What would need to change for HCC improvement to become a priority?"

**[If potential but timing issue]**

"Here's what I'd suggest. Let's do this in two phases:
- Phase 1 (now, zero cost): 7-day analysis of your data. We identify your specific gaps + revenue opportunity
- Phase 2 (next budget cycle): Full implementation when you have budget

That way, when you're pitching the CFO for budget, you'll have hard data. Sound fair?"

**Key point:** Don't lose the deal over timing. Buy time with low-cost pilots.

**Your response:**
