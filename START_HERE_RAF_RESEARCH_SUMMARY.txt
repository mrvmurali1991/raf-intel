CORE RAF CALCULATION FORMULA
================================================================================

Total RAF = Demographic + Σ(HCC Coefficients) + Σ(Disease Interactions) + Count Modifiers

Adjusted RAF = Raw RAF / Normalization Factor

EXAMPLE: 72-year-old male with diabetes, CHF, and CKD
- Demographic: 0.527
- HCC 20 (Diabetes): 0.166
- HCC 85 (Heart Failure): 0.360
- HCC 328 (CKD): 0.095
- Interaction (Diabetes + CHF): +0.112
- Interaction (CHF + CKD): +0.189
- HCC Count (5+ HCCs): +0.050
- Raw RAF: 1.499
- Normalized (÷1.050): 1.428
- Annual Payment: $12,500 × 1.428 = $17,850

V24/V28 TRANSITION (Medicare Advantage):
- PY2024: 67% V24 + 33% V28
- PY2025: 33% V24 + 67% V28
- PY2026+: 100% V28

NORMALIZATION FACTORS:
- 2024: 1.030
- 2025: 1.045
- 2026: ~1.050

TYPICAL RAF VALUES:
- New enrollee: 0.45-0.75 (demographics only)
- Healthy continuing: 0.70-0.90
- Sick continuing: 1.20-2.50
- Institutional: +20-40% higher baseline

FINANCIAL IMPACT:
- RAF difference of 0.01: $120-200 annually per member
- New vs. continuing enrollee: 50-75% payment difference
- V28 transition: -3.12% average (billions saved to Medicare)

WHAT MAKES RAF CALCULATION COMPLEX
================================================================================

1. HIERARCHY RULES
   --> Some HCC diagnoses suppress others (more severe wins)
   --> But interactions apply INDEPENDENTLY of hierarchy

2. MULTIPLE MODELS
   --> V24, V28, V22 (PACE), ESRD, HHS-HCC (ACA)
   --> Each with different coefficients and rules

3. CALCULATION DIFFERENCES
   --> New enrollees: demographics only (no HCC credit)
   --> Continuing enrollees: full HCC calculation
   --> Institutional: 20-40% higher demographic baseline

4. ANNUAL CHANGES
   --> New coefficients released April each year
   --> New ICD-10 codes October each year
   --> Normalization factors change yearly

5. TRANSITION PERIODS
   --> V24/V28 blend in 2024-2025
   --> PACE has different slower transition (2026-2029)

================================================================================
CRITICAL RULES TO GET RIGHT
================================================================================

RULE 1: One HCC per Category per Year
  WRONG: Count same diagnosis 4 times for 4 visits = 0.360 × 4
  RIGHT: Count once = 0.360

RULE 2: Apply Interactions Regardless of Hierarchy
  WRONG: If diabetes suppressed by cancer, skip interaction
  RIGHT: Interaction applies even if diagnosis suppressed

RULE 3: New Enrollees Get Zero HCC Credit
  WRONG: New enrollee with chronic conditions gets HCC credit
  RIGHT: New enrollee gets demographics only until 12 months data

RULE 4: Always Apply Normalization Factor
  WRONG: Raw RAF = 2.100 is final score
  RIGHT: 2.100 / 1.050 = 2.000 (normalized)

RULE 5: Different Models for Different Programs
  WRONG: Use MA model for ACA patient
  RIGHT: Use HHS-HCC for ACA, CMS-HCC V28 for MA

RULE 6: Institutional Gets Higher Baseline
  WRONG: SNF patient gets same demographic as community
  RIGHT: SNF demographic coefficient × 1.20-1.40

RULE 7: ESRD Kidney Disease HCCs = Zero
  WRONG: ESRD dialysis patient gets HCC 327 (CKD Stage 5) credit
  RIGHT: HCC 327 = 0.0 for all ESRD patients

RULE 8: Blend Calculations for Transition Years
  WRONG: PY2024 = 33% V24 + 67% V28
  RIGHT: PY2024 = 67% V24 + 33% V28

WORKED EXAMPLE: STEP-BY-STEP CALCULATION
================================================================================

Patient: 78-year-old female, community, non-dual, continuing enrollee
Diagnoses: Diabetes, Heart Failure, CKD Stage 3B, COPD, Hypertension

Step 1: Demographic (V28 age 78F): 0.650

Step 2: HCC Coefficients
- HCC 106 (Hypertension): 0.086
- HCC 111 (COPD): 0.441
- HCC 20 (Diabetes): 0.166
- HCC 85 (CHF): 0.360
- HCC 328 (CKD): 0.095
Sum: 1.148

Step 3: Disease Interactions
- Diabetes + CHF: +0.112
- CHF + CKD: +0.189
- Diabetes + CKD: +0.095
Sum: 0.396

Step 4: HCC Count Modifier (5 HCCs): +0.050

Step 5: Raw RAF
0.650 + 1.148 + 0.396 + 0.050 = 2.244

Step 6: Normalized (÷1.050)
2.244 / 1.050 = 2.138

Step 7: Annual Payment
$12,500 benchmark × 2.138 = $26,725
