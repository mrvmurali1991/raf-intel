QUICK REFERENCE: KEY DATA POINTS FROM RESEARCH
================================================================================

RAF Calculation:
  Formula: RAF = Demographic Value + Σ(HCC Weights) + Interaction Factors
  Example simple patient (diabetes): RAF 1.026 → $12,312/year
  Example complex patient (CHF, CKD, diabetes+, COPD): RAF 2.551 → $30,612/year

Common HCC Weights (at $12K base rate):
  - CHF (HCC 85): 0.368 → $4,416/year per patient
  - COPD (HCC 111): 0.346 → $4,152/year per patient
  - Diabetes with complications (HCC 18): 0.307 → $3,684/year per patient
  - CKD Stage 3b (HCC 135): 0.298 → $3,576/year per patient

Industry Benchmarks:
  - National average RAF: 1.08
  - High-performing organizations: 1.15+
  - Baseline recapture rate (no program): 65-75%
  - With pre-visit workflows: 90-95%
  - In-encounter gap resolution: 90%+ success rate

Financial Impact:
  - 5% RAF improvement (10K population) = $6.48M annual revenue
  - 1% improvement = $141-$282 PMPY per patient
  - Implementation cost Year 1: $280-400K
  - ROI: 10-24x return on investment
  - Payback period: 1-2 months

Patient Engagement Effectiveness:
  - SMS/text: 45% engagement rate
  - Email: 25% engagement rate
  - Phone: 35% engagement rate
  - In-clinic prompts: 90%+ effectiveness

MEAT Documentation Standard:
  Requirement: Minimum ONE of the following per chronic condition per year
  - M (Monitoring): Vital signs, symptoms, patient report
  - E (Evaluation): Test results, exam findings
  - A (Assessment): Status, stability, control
  - T (Treatment): Medications, adjustments, management plans

SDOH Integration:
  - Social determinants account for 80% of health outcomes
  - New code G0136 effective Jan 2026 ($15-25 reimbursement per assessment)
  - Z-codes document SDOH but are currently non-billable
  - Integration into official RAF models expected 2027-2030

HIPAA/Privacy Requirements:
  - RAF data is Protected Health Information (PHI)
  - Access controls: Role-based access minimum
  - Encryption: TLS 1.2+ in transit; encrypted at rest
  - Business Associate Agreements: Required for all vendors
  - Deidentification: Remove 18 identifiers per Safe Harbor method

RADV Audit Expansion (Critical Risk):
  - CMS coders: 40 → 2,000 by Sept 2025
  - MA plans audited: 60/year → 550+ annually by 2026
  - Industry error rate: 9.5%
  - Best practice error rate: <5%
  - Common findings: Undocumented diagnoses, hierarchical violations
